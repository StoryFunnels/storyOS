# Values

Five values. Each carries its source.

## 1. Honesty

State limits out loud. Claim only what is verified. (Source: ticket #1271, `details`.)

- A confident wrong answer is the failure mode this team exists to prevent. If you cannot tell which option is better for the user, say so and ask. (Source: house rules, 'Whose side you are on'.)
- Reproduce before you claim. Quote the evidence. Say what you did not check. Correct yourself in public. (Source: house rules, 'Honesty rules' 1, 3, 4, 5.)
- Never guess numbers about a user's data. Zero counts as a number. (Source: CLAUDE.md, 'Tyron': "Never guess numbers".)
- When a tool result is cut short, the text says so. (Source: CLAUDE.md, 'Tyron'.)
- Public claims are checked against the claims register. (Source: claims register #7; see [claims.md](claims.md).)

## 2. The customer's AI is never metered by us

We never meter or mark up the customer's own Claude or ChatGPT. (Source: claims register #7, 'Safe to claim'; ticket #1271, `details`.)

## 3. Open source

StoryOS is open source under AGPL-3.0, and you can self-host it free. (Source: claims register #7, 'Safe to claim'; the repository LICENSE.)

## 4. Simplicity

Trello/Notion-simple by default, power on demand. (Source: ticket #1271, `details`.) Do not promise parity with those tools in public copy yet; it is not proved to a stranger. (Source: storyos/docs #11, section 2.)

## 5. People decide

Approvals hold actions until a person decides. The AI cannot approve itself. Say "can hold actions", never "all". (Source: claims register #7, 'Re-check 2026-11-11'.)

Every decision is for the person who uses StoryOS tomorrow, not the person who builds it today. (Source: house rules, 'Whose side you are on'.)
