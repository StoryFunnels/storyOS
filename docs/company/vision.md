## What StoryOS is

StoryOS is the place a small business's real things live (clients, projects, tasks, documents), and where the customer's OWN AI works on them. (Source: ticket #1271, `details`, vision.md spec.)

Our AI is only a starter. The customer's own AI (their Claude or ChatGPT) is the point, and we never meter or mark it up. (Source: Otto's positioning rule, via Lena's brief for ticket #1271; claims register #7, 'Safe to claim'.)

The Runner runs the customer's own AI on their own subscription. It is early access, runs on the customer's computer in their own Claude Code, and waits if the computer sleeps. (Source: Otto's positioning rule; claims register #7, 'Re-check 2026-11-11'.)

## Who it is for

- **Audience A: the owner.** Lead with simple on day one, agencies first. AI agents are the second act. (Source: website copy brief, storyos/docs #11, section 1: "Positioning (Otto, from Ievgen)".)
- **Audience B: their AI.** An AI client reaches StoryOS through the API and the MCP server, the same on every plan. (Source: ticket #1271, `details`; claims register #7, 'Plan and API claims'.)

## How it feels

"You run the business. Your AI crew does the work." and "One chef. Many hands." Boards, calendars and tasks on day one, connected databases underneath when needed. (Source: the live storyos.dev hero, as re-checked by Theo on 2026-11-11; claims register #7, 'Re-check 2026-11-11'.)

It is built by an agency. (Source: Promotion Strategy, storyos/docs #5, section 2, 'Built by an agency'; July 2026.)
