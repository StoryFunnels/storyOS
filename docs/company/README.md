# Company docs: read this first

Read these before any product or copy decision, whether you are a person or an agent. They hold the "why" that used to live in scattered tickets and memory. (Source: ticket #1271, `details`.)

Ievgen's brief for this folder: "a section inside the repo with docs about our vision, our values, etc. Not only for the website, but also agentic accessible inside the REPO." (Source: ticket #1271, `source`.)

## The files

- [vision.md](vision.md): what StoryOS is, who it is for.
- [values.md](values.md): honesty, the customer's AI, open source, simplicity, people decide.
- [product-principles.md](product-principles.md): rulings we keep re-ruling. Read before you design a gate, a color, a pack or a pricing line.
- [voice.md](voice.md): how we write, and how Quill speaks.
- [claims.md](claims.md): what we may say publicly. A dated mirror, not the live source.

## Five rules for agents

1. The StoryOS backlog and docs library are the live source. These files are dated snapshots. If a file and the live record disagree, the live record wins and you say so on the ticket. (Source: house rules, 'Honesty rules' 5 and 6; claims register, storyos/docs #7.)
2. A public claim must be live in production, not only merged to main. (Source: claims register #7, 'Rulings added 2026-10-24'.)
3. Say what you did not check. An unstated gap reads as a cleared check. (Source: house rules, 'Honesty rules' 3.)
4. Choose the person who uses StoryOS tomorrow over the person who builds it today, and say what that costs. (Source: house rules, 'Whose side you are on'.)
5. Never name the reference tool, a competitor or a client in public text. (Source: CLAUDE.md, hard rule 5; Quill voice guide, storyos/docs #8, section 1.)
