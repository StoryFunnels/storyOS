# Voice

## How we write

- Short sentences, concrete nouns. (Source: ticket #1271, `details`, voice.md spec.)
- Headlines and body copy use product words: records, databases, views, approvals. Kitchen words live only in drawings and captions. (Source: Quill voice guide, storyos/docs #8, section 1.)
- One exception: the About origin story may use ravens and the kitchen. Any product claim inside it uses product words and must be true. Ievgen approves the text. (Source: storyos/docs #8, Otto's answers 2026-10-24.)
- No buzzwords: no "AI-powered", "supercharge", "revolutionary". (Source: storyos/docs #8, section 3.)
- Never name a competitor, a client or the reference tool. (Source: storyos/docs #8, section 3; CLAUDE.md, hard rule 5.)
- Do not use the number of view types, or the MCP tool count, until the site agrees with the product. (Source: claims register #7, 'Careful' and 'Corrections from Nils'.)

## Retire list

Phrases we no longer use. Each is false or unverified. (Source: claims register #7, sections named below.)

- "No per-seat pricing", bare. Allowed only with the qualifier. ('Pricing frame 2026-10-24'.)
- "Agents work while you sleep". Say "Automations run on schedule while you sleep". ('Otto's verdicts 2026-10-24'.)
- "Prepaid credits at near cost". ('Re-check 2026-11-11'.)

## Quill

- One dry remark per scene, in italic serif. She comments on the work; she never sells it. (Source: storyos/docs #8, section 1.)
- Her lines carry no prices, numbers, plan names or feature names. (Source: storyos/docs #8, section 3.)
- Never beside plan cards, prices or the comparison table, and never on checkout, upgrade or payment screens. (Source: storyos/docs #8, Pricing decision; #11, 2026-11-05.)
- Silent or plain when something is lost, broken, billed wrongly or insecure. She never blames the user. (Source: storyos/docs #8, section 3.)
- No scene may show a feature that is not live in production. (Source: storyos/docs #8, section 1.)
- Gold appears only where an agent acts. (Source: storyos/docs #8, section 1.) Dara's current sketches may be used publicly and swapped later. (Source: storyos/docs #8, Ievgen's decision 2026-11-05.)
- No gold on her body or props: only in ticket #1271's spec. (Unsourced beyond it; see hand-back.)
