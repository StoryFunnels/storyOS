## Safe to claim (register, 2026-10-23, re-checked 2026-11-11)

- Plans: Free $0 (2 editors, 100 runs a month), Pro $29 (3 editors, 1,000 runs), Business $99 (5 editors, 10,000 runs); extra editors $12 a month. ('Safe to claim'; 'Re-check 2026-11-11'.)
- Unlimited records on every plan. Do not say "ever" or "never cap". ('Plan and API claims'.)
- Viewers and guests are always free; you pay only for people who edit. ('Rulings 2026-10-24'.)
- Your own Claude or ChatGPT is never metered or marked up. ('Safe to claim'.)
- Self-host free under AGPL-3.0; do not say no other license exists. ('Safe to claim'.)
- The MCP server works with Claude and ChatGPT; the same API and MCP on every plan. ('Safe to claim'; 'Plan and API claims'.)
- Business Packs install with sample data. Nine exist; "a whole agency in one install" is fair for Agency OS. The pack agent drafts, you approve. ('Otto's answers 2026-10-24'.)
- Export your whole workspace, every row and every link, in one zip. Admin only; leaves out personal spaces, system databases and deleted records. ('Otto's verdicts 2026-10-24'.)
- Automations run on schedule while you sleep. ('Otto's verdicts 2026-10-24'.)
- Template agents and Deal Desk agents install switched off. The Runner is early access, runs on your computer and waits if it sleeps. Approvals wait for a person ("can hold actions"). ('Re-check 2026-11-11'.)

## Careful: say it this way

- "Same features, different scale and history" (history is 0 / 1 / 7 / 30 days). ('Careful'.)
- "Priced per workspace, not per seat" only with: every plan includes editors, extra editors are $12, viewers and guests are always free. ('Pricing frame'.)
- "Plans differ in automation runs, included editors and history; records and storage are not metered." ('Plan and API claims'.)
- Over-quota runs are "Skipped (quota)"; say "next month", not "the 1st". ('Otto's answers'.)

## Unverified: do not use in a campaign

30-day no-card Pro trial; Stripe checkout live; lifecycle emails; PDF export; 80% plan-limit email; public licensing and CLA policy; Enterprise SSO/SAML, dedicated instance, SLA and DPA; the MCP tool count; any star count. ('Unverified'; 'Re-check 2026-11-11'.)

## Do NOT promise

- Social posting from StoryOS; promote no Social Command Center.
- Restricted or private spaces beyond what ships; Linear migration switch.
- "Trello/Linear/Notion-grade out of the box" (ticket #1022).
- Back-button behaviour and click-title-to-open (merged, not deployed at the 2026-10-24 check).
- "Near cost" for managed AI credits: the code sets a 10x markup (provisional). Say "optional prepaid credits for hosted AI". ('Re-check 2026-11-11'.)
- "Every plan has every feature"; "no per-seat pricing", bare; "full-fidelity export". ('Careful'; 'Pricing frame'; 'Plan and API claims'.)
